What the numbers show

Occupancy across Westhaven's 2,014 tracked berths held in a tight 84–85% band for most of the period covered, touching a five-year high of 86.4% in April 2025. Since then it has slid steadily, and by September 2026 stood at 80.9% — the lowest point in the dataset and a decline that has accelerated over the last eighteen months.

  • Pile berths are in freefall. Occupancy dropped from 41% in 2021 to under 20% by 2026 — the steepest, most sustained decline of any berth type, and the single largest drag on the marina-wide average.
  • Marina Berths, the core of the fleet, are still healthy but softening. The 1,510 standard berths — three-quarters of all inventory — ran in the low-to-mid 90s throughout, easing from a 94.4% high in 2023–24 to 91.6% so far in 2026.
  • Multihull and Alongside berths moved the other way. Multihull occupancy climbed from 73% to a 96–99% run through 2024–25 before a mild 2026 pullback; Alongside berths went from essentially empty to fully booked from 2023 onward.
  • Seasonality is real but modest. Occupancy is consistently a little softer in the southern-hemisphere winter (Jun–Aug, ~83.5%) and firmer in early summer (Nov–Dec, ~84.9%) — a swing of under a point and a half.
